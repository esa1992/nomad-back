## Phase Goal

**As a** guest, **I want to** start an authoritative Alchiki match from a short room code, rematch, rejoin after a brief drop, or leave and forfeit, **so that** I can play a friend without Quick Match, Ranked, or a bot sitting in their seat.

ROADMAP Goal restated into story slots (no new capability). This plan is the allowed Wave 0 cycle-break slice: after it, the bot catalog path still works and two-player types can be added without a Modulith cycle.

## Artifacts this phase produces

This plan produces the acyclic GameEngine SPI and session-owned MatchStatus. Later plans add rooms, lobby, WebSocket play, leave-vs-human, rematch, and 30s reconnect.

- `com.nomadgames.session.MatchStatus` — moved from games; adds HOST_WIN, JOINER_WIN
- `GameEngine` methods: `start`, `startPrivate`, `applyThrow`, `nextBotThrow`, `resolve`, `forfeitThrowIfExpired`, `turnClock`, `matchLimit`, `hardCap`
- `ScoreClock`, `PrivateTable` records in session
- `AlchikiEngine` implements the full SPI; `ScriptedBot` stays inside games.alchiki.internal
- `MatchService` with zero games.alchiki imports
