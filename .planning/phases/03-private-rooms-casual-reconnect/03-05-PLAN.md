## Phase Goal

**As a** guest, **I want to** start an authoritative Alchiki match from a short room code, rematch, rejoin after a brief drop, or leave and forfeit, **so that** I can play a friend without Quick Match, Ranked, or a bot sitting in their seat.

This slice: server can score a private throw over raw WebSocket with two sakas (D-37, D-38). Flutter table wiring is 03-06.

- `MatchSessionRegistry` heap map matchId → open sessions (reconnect policy 03-09)
