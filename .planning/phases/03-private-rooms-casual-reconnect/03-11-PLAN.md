## Phase Goal

**As a** guest, **I want to** start an authoritative Alchiki match from a short room code, rematch, rejoin after a brief drop, or leave and forfeit, **so that** I can play a friend without Quick Match, Ranked, or a bot sitting in their seat.

This slice: close VERIFICATION truth 2 / CR-03 — concurrent Ready and join no longer lose the kickoff matchId.

Root cause: **plan-deficiency**. 03-04 specified sequential `RoomIT#bothReadyStartsJoinerTurn` and unlocked `findById` / `findByCode`. The intended both-tap was never a failing test, so the executor shipped a correct sequential path.
